# Hi Again — Naming & Brand Research Reference

Project: Hi Again networking app | Reference date: 30 October 2026 | Status: Provisional brand selected, clearance pending

## 1. Executive summary

Hi Again emerged as the preferred name for a networking app designed to turn real-world encounters into lasting connections.

The app addresses a familiar problem: people meet at conferences, professional events and social gatherings, exchange details, but rarely follow up.

The proposed solution is a QR-based profile exchange that saves connections and makes it easier to remember, reconnect with and follow up with people.

The preferred domain is `hiagain.link`, which Cloudflare displayed as available for $7.20/year, including renewal.

Initial trademark checks were encouraging, but subsequent research identified an existing social networking app called HiAgain and a similarly named hackathon project. After reconsidering the risks, the decision was not to abandon the name prematurely.

The current position is to retain Hi Again as the leading candidate, consider securing the inexpensive domain, and undertake further clearance before significant public branding investment.

## 2. Product vision and positioning

The app is intended to bridge the gap between meeting someone and maintaining the relationship.

The established MVP direction is:

- Users have shareable networking profiles accessible by QR code.
- Opening someone's QR profile can save that person within Hi Again.
- LinkedIn and Google Sign-In are the two intended authentication methods.
- Users can optionally open someone's LinkedIn profile to connect manually.
- The product should ultimately encourage meaningful follow-up rather than simply accumulate contacts.

The branding should feel human, friendly, modern and approachable, rather than like a conventional corporate CRM or digital business card.

Core brand proposition: The first meeting should be the beginning of a relationship, not the end.

| Mingle    | Friendly and social                               | Less professional                         |

Other exploratory names included Folo, Follo, FollowOn, Relay, Loop, Mesh, Nodi, Kivo and Rapport.

### How the preference evolved

First: Followup. This was considered a strong functional name because it clearly describes the problem being solved. However, its associations with sales software and productivity tools were a weakness.

Second: Hi Again. This was preferred because it sounds like something people naturally say to someone they've met before. It supports a more human, social identity and a broader long-term product vision.

The proposed display name became Hi Again, with `hiagain` as a compact written brand form.

## 5. Domain research

The original candidate was `hi-again.link`.

It was considered suitable because `.link` directly reflects the app's purpose, and the hyphen makes the two words easy to recognise.

The disadvantage was the need to explain the hyphen when sharing the address verbally.

A subsequent Cloudflare screenshot showed:

Preferred domain

# hiagain.link

## $7.20/year

Displayed registration and renewal price

Cloudflare search result supplied by the user; availability and pricing not independently rechecked.

The unhyphenated domain became the preferred option because it is cleaner, easier to communicate and better suited to personal profile URLs such as `hiagain.link/tek`.

Decision: Prefer `hiagain.link` over `hi-again.link`.

Not confirmed: Whether the domain has actually been purchased.

## 7. Existing-brand research

Two notable projects were identified during the conversation.

### HiAgain — Never Lose Touch

Existing social networking application

Website: [hiagain.co](https://www.hiagain.co/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

App: [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hiagain-never-lose-touch/id6758457241?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Earlier research identified an iPhone app called HiAgain — Never Lose Touch, associated with Rushil Patel and launched in February 2026.

Its focus is reconnecting classmates, alumni and existing social networks through profiles, friend requests and related features.

Relevance: The name is effectively identical, and both products operate in social networking.

Distinction: Hi Again's proposed MVP focuses on meeting new people in person, QR-based exchange and subsequent follow-up, rather than rediscovering existing acquaintances.

This was considered the most significant brand-overlap concern.

### hi again — DubHacks project

Project: [View Devpost submission](https://devpost.com/software/hi-again?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

A project attributed to Kingston Du was identified, centred on using NFC-enabled student IDs to exchange social profiles, capture meeting context and remember new contacts.

Its functionality resembles the Hi Again MVP more closely than the alumni app does.

However, it appeared to be a hackathon submission rather than an established commercial service. Its current commercial status was not verified.

Assessment: Relevant competitive research, but not by itself a strong reason to abandon the brand.

## 8. Risk assessment and revised conclusion

The initial reaction to the competing HiAgain app was that the naming overlap was a significant concern and might justify changing the name.

This assessment was subsequently reconsidered.

The final position was more nuanced:

| Risk                                      | Working assessment                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Registered UK trademark conflict          | Not identified in preliminary searches |
| Unregistered trademark/passing-off rights | Unresolved                             |
| Customer confusion                        | Moderate concern                       |
| Search visibility and discoverability     | Moderate concern                       |
| Domain availability                       | Positive at time of screenshot         |
| Small private MVP                         | Manageable naming risk                 |
| Major public launch under the brand       | Requires further diligence             |

These are qualitative judgments from the discussion, not legal findings.

A key distinction was established between legal risk and commercial branding risk. Another app using the same name does not automatically prohibit use, but could make discovery, differentiation and future expansion more difficult.

## 9. Decisions and outstanding actions

Decided

Hi Again remains the leading brand candidate. `hiagain.link` is the preferred domain. The product should emphasise real-world connections and meaningful follow-up, with a friendly, non-corporate identity.

Not yet decided or verified

Domain purchase, final tagline, final visual identity, complete trademark clearance, competitor UK market presence, and long-term public brand commitment.

### Recommended next steps

1. Secure `hiagain.link` if still available at the displayed price and if comfortable with the possibility of rebranding.
2. Review the existing HiAgain app's UK availability, audience, traction and trademark ownership.
3. Complete checks for active similar marks and relevant unregistered UK businesses.
4. Continue developing the MVP without substantial brand-specific marketing expenditure.
5. Obtain professional trademark clearance before a significant public launch or trademark filing.

## 10. Final project position

Working name: Hi Again\
Preferred domain: `hiagain.link`\
Brand concept: A friendly networking app that helps people turn first encounters into lasting connections.\
Status: Preferred candidate, not fully cleared.\
Strategic decision: Proceed cautiously rather than discard the name solely because similar projects exist.

The guiding principle from the discussion is that Hi Again is a strong product name worth preserving if the remaining legal and commercial risks prove acceptable.
